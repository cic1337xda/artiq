Privacy Policy

ArtiQ Smart AR Physiotherapy • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

ArtiQ collects only the operational data necessary to manage physiotherapy sessions: administrator usernames, verified clinic email addresses for one-time password (OTP) authentication, patient session labels (display name, age, repetition counts, and timestamps), and linked ESP32 hardware MAC addresses.

2. Local Edge AI Webcam Processing

All MediaPipe hand landmark tracking and grip form evaluations run 100% locally inside your browser on your desktop or laptop. No video streams, camera frames, or facial/hand images are recorded, uploaded, or stored on any server.

3. EMG Hardware Telemetry

Live muscle activation metrics (%MVC, smoothed envelope readings, and calibration state) from the MyoWare 2.0 ESP32 node are transmitted over MQTT strictly for real-time biofeedback visualization during active clinical sessions.

4. Security & Access Controls

Administrator passwords are hashed with Bcrypt, authentication sessions rely on signed HttpOnly SameSite=Lax JWT cookies, and all email verification codes expire after 10 minutes with single-use enforcement.
